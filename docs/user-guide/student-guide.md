# NST Connect: Student guide

NST Connect is where you raise a problem (hostel, academics, library, IT and more) and follow it until it is solved.

---

## 1. In short

1. Sign up with your college email, verify it, and log in.
2. Click **New ticket**, fill in the form, and press **Send**.
3. Follow it under **My tickets**. **We do not email you about replies**, so open the ticket to check.
4. When it is **Solved** or **Closed**, you can give feedback. If you are not happy, you can **reopen** it.

---

## 2. Your account

**Sign up** with your college email (it ends in `@adypu.edu.in`). Other addresses are refused.

| Field | Required? | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full Name | Yes | 2 to 100 characters |
| Email | Yes | your college email |
| Password | Yes | at least 10 characters |

- We email you a **verification link**, valid for **24 hours**. Check spam if it does not arrive. You cannot log in until you open it.
- If the link expires, sign up again with the same email to get a new one.
- **Forgot password?** (on the login page): we email a link that works for **1 hour** and only once. Choose a new password; you are logged out everywhere else.
- Too many wrong logins in a minute are blocked for a short time. Wait a minute and try again.
- You stay logged in for about 7 days. Use **Log out** (avatar menu, top right) on shared computers.

## 3. Raising a ticket ("New ticket")

| Step | Required? | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Topic** | Yes | Click a chip, for example Hostel. Nothing is preselected. |
| **Issue** | Yes | Pick the specific problem. If a topic has only one issue, it is chosen for you. |
| **Title** | Yes | 3 to 200 characters |
| **Describe your issue** | Yes | 10 to 5,000 characters. Say what happened, where, since when, and what you tried. |
| **Attach** | **Optional** | See below. |

Press **Send**. Anything missing is shown in red and nothing is sent. After sending you see "Sent to *Hostel team*" and a **View** link.

If you see "Ticket topics are not open yet", the teams are not ready yet. Check back later.

### Attachments (optional)
- Up to **10 files**, added **when you raise the ticket**. You cannot add files later.
- Images (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP), documents (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, CSV, TXT) up to **10 MB** each. Videos (MP4, MOV, WEBM) up to **100 MB** each.
- Other types (such as zip files, programs, SVG) are not allowed. The file's real contents are checked, so renaming a file will not work.
- Files marked red cannot be sent: remove them (✕) first.
- If a file fails to upload, the ticket is still created and you are told how many files were not attached.

You only see a team name such as "Hostel team". Replies never show a person's name.

## 4. Following your tickets ("My tickets")

Tabs: **All**, **Open**, **Done** (Solved or Closed). You only see your own tickets.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Created | Raised, waiting to be given to a team member. |
| Assigned | A team member has it. |
| In progress | The team is working on it. |
| Escalated | Passed to a more senior person. Nothing for you to do. |
| Solved | The team fixed it. |
| Closed | The team finished it without a fix (for example duplicate or not possible). |
| Closed by you | You closed it yourself. |
| Reopened | You reopened it. It is back with the team. |

Open a ticket to see your description, attachments (click to download) and the **Timeline**: every step in order, including the team's replies and their note when it is Solved or Closed. Your own messages show as "You".

### You cannot send messages on an open ticket
The only message you write after sending is the reason when you **reopen**. If the team needs more information they will ask in a reply on the timeline. Put as much detail as you can in the first description. For a new problem, raise a new ticket.

### How long does it take?
The team aims to solve a ticket within **2 days**. If it is not solved in time, it is automatically passed to a more senior person and you will see "Escalated" on the timeline. You do not need to do anything.

---

## 5. Closing a ticket yourself

On an open ticket, under **Sorted out already?**, press **Close ticket** and confirm. *"Our team will stop working on it. You can reopen it later if you still need help."*

- It becomes **Closed by you**. You are not asked for feedback.
- You can reopen it later.

---

## 6. When the team finishes your ticket

The team marks it **Solved** or **Closed** and always writes you a note, shown on the timeline.

### Feedback (optional)
Choose 😊 Satisfied, 😐 Okay or 😞 Not satisfied. A comment (up to 500 characters) is optional.
- You can **change** your answer for **7 days**.
- Your answer is **anonymous**: the staff never see who you are.

### Reopen (if you are not satisfied)
1. Press **Reopen ticket**.
2. Write what is still wrong (**required**, at least 5 characters, up to 2,000).
3. Press **Reopen**. The ticket goes back to the team and a fresh timer starts.

If the page says **"This outcome is final."**, there is no reopen button. Raise a new ticket if you have a new problem.

## 7. Quick answers

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is the title optional? | No, it is required. |
| Are attachments optional? | Yes. |
| Is feedback optional? | Yes, and so is its comment. |
| Can I edit or delete a ticket after sending? | No. You can close it, or reopen it with a comment once finished. |
| Can I see who is handling my ticket? | No, only the team name. |
| Will I be told when someone replies? | Not by email. Open **My tickets** to check. |
| Can staff see my name and email? | Yes, the staff handling your ticket can. Your feedback is anonymous. |
| Emails I will get | Sign-up verification and password reset only. |
